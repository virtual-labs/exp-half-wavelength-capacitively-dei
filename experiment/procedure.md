1. Click the "Concept" tab to read the basic theory of the λ/2 resonator.
2. Click the "Formula" tab to note down the resonant frequency, Q-factor, and equivalent circuit formulas.
3. Click the "Design" tab to open the design and simulation dashboard.
4. Enter the design parameters (W, h, εr, tanδ, L, g, Wf, Lf) in the input fields.
5. Observe the resonator layout updating live in the Geometry (Top View) panel.
6. Click the "Run" button to perform the S-parameter sweep simulation.
7. Note the S11 vs S21, Group Delay, S11 Phase, and Standing Wave graphs generated.
8. Read the resonant frequency (f0), Q-factor, and bandwidth from the Results Summary.
9. Switch to the "Field" tab and move the slider to observe the E-field and H-field distribution.
10. Switch to the "Equivalent Circuit" tab to view the R_eq, L_eq, C_eq, and C_gap values with the RLC circuit diagram.
11. Change one design parameter at a time and observe its effect on the resonant frequency and Q-factor.
12. Record all observations and write a conclusion based on the results obtained.
